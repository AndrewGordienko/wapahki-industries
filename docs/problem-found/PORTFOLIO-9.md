## The Canada thesis

Canada needs substantially higher housing productivity, stronger domestic trade
and supply chains, more resilient infrastructure, and lower operating costs.

- CMHC estimates weak construction productivity added **$6–8 billion** to housing
  costs between 2019 and 2024.
- Removing internal trade barriers could add as much as **$210 billion** to the
  economy (Government of Canada).
- Canadian–US goods trade **declined in 2025** amid tariffs and uncertainty
  (Global Affairs Canada), forcing companies to diversify suppliers.

These nine tell a stronger Canada-facing story than a grab-bag of demos: build
housing faster, strengthen domestic production, cut food and freight costs,
improve healthcare access, and make critical infrastructure more resilient.
