## The four handbooks

- **A — Research and proof boundaries** (`A-research-and-proof-boundaries.md`):
  what public evidence can and cannot establish; observed fact versus inferred
  relevance; proof scope.
- **B — Problem-hypothesis and ownership testing**
  (`B-problem-hypothesis-and-ownership-testing.md`): uncertainty language,
  falsifiable hypotheses, last-real-case questions, the SPICED qualification
  order, testing ownership instead of inferring it, no premature ROI or pilot.
- **C — Cold-email construction** (`C-cold-email-construction.md`): line-level
  edits, length and structure limits, one low-effort answerable ask, positioning
  before pitch.
- **D — Sequence progression and account routing**
  (`D-sequence-progression-and-account-routing.md`): every touch must add new
  value; operator / router / economic-owner routing; reach a second stakeholder
  deliberately instead of blasting the account.

## The two governing hard rules

1. **Evidence boundary.** Public evidence may establish the company's
   environment, but it cannot establish the private workflow, its owner, its
   difficulty or its financial consequence.
2. **Follow-up value gate.** Reject any follow-up that does not add new evidence,
   a more concrete example, a useful artifact or a materially different ask.

These are promoted into the writer's brain (`playbooks/_shared.md`, the
`OUTBOUND-DOCTRINE` block and Gate 1) and registered in `docs/HANDBOOK.md`.
